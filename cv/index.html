Platforms & Tools

  • AgentSims: Agentic Simulations — agentsims.agentsnet.org LLM-driven multi-agent simulation framework with persistent state and replayable histories for reproducible academic research on collective behavior. Designed to support industry scenario analysis, strategy evaluation, and risk assessment through configurable agent environments.
  • Founders-Sim: Market Competition Simulation — founders-sim.agentsnet.org Rule-based simulation of startups vying for users in a synthetic population of 80,000 groups (100,000 people each). Social ties spread word of products, but only advertising or referrals can start free trials, after which some pay. The site replays a recorded 1,000-week run.
  • Cytolume: GPU-Accelerated Million-Cell Visualization — muyuhuatang.github.io/Cytolume WebGL2 visualization library for large single-cell embeddings: UMAP, t-SNE, and PCA layouts in 2D and 3D, lasso selection, Human Reference Atlas anatomy beside the cell map, and figure export with data provenance. Successor to Human Reference Atlas cell-embedding notebooks built with Prof. Katy Börner's group in 2024. A Python package serves the viewer from AnnData objects, locally or in Jupyter.
  • Auto-Research Monitor — muyuhuatang.github.io/auto-research-monitor Live monitor for autonomous research agents: every question, experiment, run, result, and claim joins an evidence lineage graph, and each claim is scored green, yellow, or red on five structural dimensions of its supporting evidence, such as coverage, reproducibility, and data provenance. Works alongside any agent, such as Claude Code, via an HTTP API, a Python SDK, or a watched project folder, and can replay each run step by step.
  • Founders-Sim: Multiplayer Startup Race Game — founders-game.agentsnet.org Race between startups on a new 3D planet each round, populated by 80,000 personas whose wealth and chance of joining are drawn at random. Players hire a team and raise funds; when two startups meet, the stronger acquires the other, yet fog of war hides every rival's strength.
